# tvOS-inspired local revision

This is an Android implementation informed by Apple's tvOS guidance, not an Apple framework or an exact reproduction of its rendering engine.

| Reference | Application in this build |
|---|---|
| [Apple: Focus and selection](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/focus-and-selection/) | A 7.5% focus lift, spring-driven target movement, clear illumination, preserved directional navigation, and no unsolicited focus changes. |
| [Apple: Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) | Glass is concentrated in system controls, the dock, and the focused tile; the artwork remains legible. A short light sweep and bounded directional stretch add a liquid response. |
| [Apple: Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) | Restrained light/regular/medium weights, compact tracking at display sizes, a smaller clock/date hierarchy, and clean legible interface shapes. |
| [Apple: Font license](https://developer.apple.com/fonts/) | Apple's font license excludes embedding its font in non-Apple software. Inter is bundled under its SIL Open Font License as the Android-compatible choice. |

## Motion and materials

Focus moves with a spring stiffness of 480 and damping ratio 0.88. App enlargement takes 210 ms on focus and 160 ms on release. The highlight's optical stretch and sheen settle after 280 ms. Stretch is capped at 5.5%, including distant or reverse focus jumps. Reduced-motion mode cancels the optical sweep and restores the existing short response. No decorative motion runs at rest.

The focus surface uses a diagonal rim, a faint cool edge, a restrained fill, and a transient caustic sweep. This approximates optical depth without full-screen, per-frame blur or backdrop captures. The surface remains a small hardware-backed layer.

## Background and type

The original procedural wallpaper is generated by RenderWallpaper.swift at 1920×1080. Curved light ribbons add shape, with quieter regions behind the clock and app row. It is a static 116 KB JPEG; no background animation or network request is needed. The existing screensaver palette sampler derives its glow from this new wallpaper when the theme is applied.

Inter is stored once as a variable font. Android resource families cache the selected weight and optical-size instance. The APK contains the font's license text.

## Icon-only Home

Inputs, Network, Clock, and Settings are circular icon-only controls. Accessibility names remain available without visible labels. Permanent app captions, the oversized selected-app heading, and the footer navigation hint are hidden. The five requested apps and their order are unchanged.

## Preview and limits

preview.html is an offline interaction mockup with the actual packaged font, wallpaper, control vectors, and three bundled brand cards. Its Prime Video and Hotstar artwork is representational; the native launcher uses the installed apps' banners. Click app tiles or use directional keys to explore focus, and choose StandBy to preview the clock. The preview has not been visually inspected in the agent browser: browser policy rejected local file URLs. Its script passes a Node syntax check.

The APK and theme are built locally. They have not been installed or timed on the TV. Frame pacing, focus optics, and remote navigation need an on-device check after reconnection. The existing TV installation is unchanged by this revision.
